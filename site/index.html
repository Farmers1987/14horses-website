About us

The historic walls of Vainai Palace, a renewed creative energy and an ambition for bold flavours. Today, 14Horses opens a new chapter with Chef Aleksandras Fedorovičius, a new team and contemporary Baltic - Scandinavian cuisine.

Our roots lie in the soil. At Farmers Circle in the village of Radiškis, the seasons set the rhythm and inspire ideas for the menu to come. 14Horses is part of this farm. From the soil to the creativity of the kitchen and the conversation at the table, an unbroken journey of discovery unfolds. Knowledge of what we grow, how we prepare it and why we choose it passes through our team to our guests, giving flavour its context and the experience its depth.

Nose to tail. Root to leaf.

This is how we understand respect for an ingredient: knowing its origins, revealing its character and finding value in every part. In our kitchen, seasonal, local produce meets unexpected combinations, adventurous techniques and bold flavours.

We find beauty in the natural: organic forms, textures and the traces left by time. An appreciation of imperfection, close to the philosophy of wabi-sabi, allows our historic setting and contemporary gastronomy to speak the same language.

At our table, this language becomes flavour - bold, distinctive and memorable.

Farmers Circle

At Farmers Circle, everything begins with living soil.

Founded in 2018 by Danish entrepreneur Niels Peter Pretzmann, this organic farm in Radiškis, Ukmergė district, nurtures the connection between soil, plants, animals and people. From the outset, it has grown around the belief that good food begins with a responsible relationship with nature. Here, the seasons set the pace, and every decision considers both the harvest to come and the land that will sustain it.

Grains grow in the fields, seasonal vegetables and culinary herbs fill the gardens, and Angus cattle graze the pastures. The farm’s produce also includes organic eggs and poultry, buckwheat, oats and flour. This diversity creates an interconnected system in which crop cultivation, market gardening and livestock farming support one another.

Part of the harvest takes on a new form right here, in the farm’s own production kitchen. Vegetables are fermented and made into sauces, spreads and condiments, while the flavours of the seasons are preserved in jams, syrups and vinegars. The short distance between garden and kitchen allows freshly harvested produce to be processed promptly, preserving its character and finding value even in ingredients with an imperfect appearance.

Care for the soil connects every aspect of the farm. Regenerative practices - reduced tillage, crop rotation, cover cropping, rotational grazing and composting - help restore soil vitality and support biodiversity. Organic matter returns to the soil through compost, nourishing future harvests. The end of one season becomes the beginning of the next.

Farmers Circle is also a place for gathering and discovery. Its very name invites growers, chefs, like-minded people and curious guests into a shared circle, united by a desire to understand where their food comes from. Farm tours, educational activities and cooking experiences open a window onto everyday farming and the work behind each ingredient.

This journey continues at Red Brick, the farm’s restaurant, where the harvest becomes a seasonal gastronomic experience. Event and meeting spaces, accommodation and a farm shop invite guests to stay a little longer - to observe, taste and settle into the rhythm of the place.

From soil to harvest. From experience to knowledge shared. This is how the Farmers Circle turns.

Restaurant policy

Restaurant Rules and Reservation Terms

Reservations and Dining Duration

Tables are reserved for 2 hours. Longer visits must be arranged in advance. Reserved tables are held for 15 minutes after the reservation time. Please inform the restaurant team if you are running late. Reservations will be cancelled if guests arrive more than 15 minutes late without prior notice.

Reservation Deposit and Cancellation Terms

A deposit of €20 per person is required to confirm a reservation. This amount will be credited towards the final bill. For cancellations made at least 24 hours before the reservation time, the deposit will be refunded. For later cancellations or no-shows, the deposit is non-refundable. The same cancellation and refund terms apply to prepaid tasting events.

Group Reservations

Reservations for groups of 8 or more guests are arranged individually. Groups may choose dishes from the current restaurant menu or a pre-agreed event menu.

Children

Children are welcome at the restaurant. High chairs are available. We do not offer a separate children’s menu.

Pets

Pets are welcome both inside the restaurant and on the terrace. The accompanying guest is responsible for their pet’s care and behaviour. Pets must not disturb other guests or interfere with restaurant operations.

Allergies and Dietary Requirements

Please notify the restaurant of any allergies, food intolerances or specific dietary requirements at least 3 days before your visit. Menu adaptations and specific adjustments are agreed individually with the restaurant team.

Kitchen Opening Hours

The kitchen is open until 21:00 Tuesday-Thursday and until 22:00 on Fridays and Saturdays.

Bringing Your Own Cake or Wine

Guests may bring their own cake or wine only by prior arrangement with the restaurant team. Additional charges apply and will be communicated when arrangements are made.

Dress Code

The recommended dress code is smart casual.

Payment and Service Charge

The restaurant accepts card payments only. Cash is not accepted. A service charge applies. The amount and applicable terms are stated on the restaurant menu.

Accessibility

The restaurant is accessible to guests using wheelchairs and those with pushchairs. Please inform us before your visit if you require additional assistance.
