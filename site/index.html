About us

We sometimes call 14horses a travelling restaurant. This is our third home — and this time, we have reined in our horses and settled in Senatorių Pasažas for good.

What has never changed is the thread: Lithuanian produce reaches the plate in the rhythm nature sets. Our own regenerative farm, Farmers Circle, and the farmers we work with all year keep us honest about the season.

The whole dinner is packed into a four-step, fixed-price menu you choose freely — snacks, starter, main course and dessert — that shows the full picture of the 14horses philosophy, rooted in local cooking.

Restaurant policy

Good to know before you visit.

Reservations & cancellation

We recommend booking a table, but walk-ins are always welcome when we have room. If your plans change, please cancel or move your booking at least 24 hours in advance, using the link in your confirmation email or by contacting us. For no-shows and late cancellations we may charge €30 per guest. We hold your table for 15 minutes after the booked time.

Allergies & dietary needs

Please tell us about allergies or dietary needs when you book, so the kitchen can prepare. The menu follows the season and what the farm delivers, so most courses can be adapted for vegetarian guests and common allergies. Our kitchen handles all common allergens, so we cannot guarantee a completely allergen-free meal.

Children

Children are welcome. There is no separate children’s menu, but the kitchen is happy to prepare smaller portions. High chairs are available on request.

Groups & private dining

For groups of 8 or more, please contact us directly. Larger groups are served a set menu, and we may ask for a deposit to confirm the booking. The whole restaurant can be booked for private events.

Payment & service charge

A 10% service charge is added to the final bill. We accept all major payment cards. Please note that we do not accept cash.

Gift vouchers

Gift vouchers for 14horses are sold online by Senatorių Pasažas, in any amount from €20 to €1,000: buy a gift voucher. Vouchers are valid for 12 months from purchase, can be used in all our shops and restaurants in Senatorių Pasažas, and cannot be exchanged for cash.
